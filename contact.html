How do I place an order?

You can place an order by calling us at +91 98765 43210, WhatsApp us, or send us an email. We'll process your order within 24 hours and provide you with payment and delivery details.

What is the shelf life of your products?

Our malt flour has a shelf life of 6 months when stored in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. We recommend consuming within 3 months for optimal freshness and nutrition.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer attractive bulk discounts for orders above 5kg. Perfect for large families, offices, or community groups. Contact us for special bulk pricing.

Is it safe for people with allergies?

Our product contains nuts, seeds, and grains. If you have specific allergies, please consult with your healthcare provider and review our complete ingredient list before consuming.

How should I consume Mirthu Poshan?

Mix 2-3 tablespoons (30-40g) with warm milk or water. You can add honey, jaggery, or fruits for taste. Best consumed in the morning or as an evening snack.
