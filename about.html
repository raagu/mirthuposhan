About Mirthu Poshan

Discover the story behind India's most trusted homemade malt flour

Our Journey

Born from ancient Indian wisdom and modern nutritional science, Mirthu Poshan represents generations of knowledge about the power of natural grains and their healing properties.

Our journey began with a simple belief: nature provides everything we need for optimal health. Drawing inspiration from traditional Indian nutrition practices and Ayurvedic principles, we carefully selected 42 powerful ingredients - each chosen for its unique nutritional profile and health benefits.

What started as a family recipe has now become a mission to bring wholesome, natural nutrition to every home. Every batch of Mirthu Poshan is crafted with love, ensuring that the ancient wisdom of our ancestors reaches your family in its purest form.
