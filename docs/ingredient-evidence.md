# Ingredient evidence

Reviewed on 31 October 2026. All 42 listed recipe entries were reviewed; whole/split and colour variants remain separate. The recipe identities and naming corrections are owner-provided. General nutrient descriptions come from the primary references below; they are not a measured nutrition panel for Mirthu Poshan.

Bellar is the owner-confirmed whole avare kalu form, and Bellar Dal is the split avare bele form. TNAU supports lablab identity and general pulse composition; precise split-form nutrient quantities are deliberately omitted. Barli was confirmed by the owner as barley, which contains gluten. The two retail links supplied for Bellar naming could not be retrieved by the web tool and are not used as scientific evidence.

## Verification method

- ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017 was downloaded and its composition tables inspected. Reference names identify the matched food/form; no whole-seed numerical analysis is assigned to a split form.
- ICAR-IIMR supports millet aliases and the conservative composition descriptions, including Baragu/proso, Saame/little and Oodhalu/barnyard. General disease-treatment claims from source pages were not copied.
- NIH ODS food-source tables support almond/sunflower vitamin E, walnut/flax ALA and pumpkin-seed magnesium. ALA is distinguished from EPA/DHA.
- Cucumber seed oil was checked against the primary study indexed as PMID 23397724. The original hydration claim was removed.
- USDA FDC 169407 was retrieved directly via the public USDA API: dried watermelon seed kernels contain protein and fat. The human-readable source link is on the ingredient page.
- The USDA handbook entry for dry tapioca pearls supports the conservative starch comparison for sabudana; it is not used to assert a botanical origin for the purchased ingredient.
- TNAU culinary-spice references support aroma/flavour descriptions for cardamom and cumin. Fenugreek claims were checked against NCCIH evidence limitations.

## Limits

The repository does not establish proportions, batch nutrition, clinical outcomes, infant suitability, product shelf life or certifications. Finished-product claims require owner-supplied, applicable evidence. No such claims are added to structured data.
