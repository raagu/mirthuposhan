The Crafting Cycle

Sourcing

We carefully select premium quality grains, millets, seeds, and cereals from trusted organic farmers across India.

Cleaning & Sorting

Each ingredient undergoes thorough cleaning and quality inspection to ensure only the best make it into our blend.

Traditional Roasting

We roast ingredients to develop their flavour before blending.

Perfect Blending

Our recipe brings together 42 listed ingredients in a homemade malt flour.

Fresh Packaging

Each batch is immediately packed in air-tight containers to preserve freshness and deliver farm-to-table quality.
